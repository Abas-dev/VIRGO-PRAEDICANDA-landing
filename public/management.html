Our Leadership

Experienced Leadership for Excellence

At Virgo Praedicanda International School, our management team brings together decades of educational experience, visionary leadership, and unwavering commitment to student success. Each member of our leadership team is dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of academic excellence while fostering a nurturing, inclusive environment where every child can thrive.

Our leaders work collaboratively to ensure that VPIS continues to be a place where students develop not only academically but also in character, creativity, and confidence. They are committed to providing the resources, support, and guidance needed for our students to become global champions.

Our Commitment

Leading with Purpose

Our management team is united by a shared commitment to the mission and vision of Virgo Praedicanda International School. We believe that effective leadership is about serving our students, supporting our faculty, and partnering with families to create an environment where every child can achieve their fullest potential.

Through collaborative decision-making, continuous improvement, and strategic planning, our leaders ensure that VPIS remains at the forefront of educational excellence, preparing our students to become confident, compassionate, and capable global citizens.

Leadership is about making others better as a result of your presence and making sure that impact lasts in your absence.
