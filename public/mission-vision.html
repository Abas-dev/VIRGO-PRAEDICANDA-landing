Our Vision

Our vision is to raise children with strong, personable character equipped with knowledge that can compete globally. We inspire and nurture excellent children for global impact — developing confident, compassionate leaders who are rooted in faith and ready to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

We envision a future where every graduate of Virgo Praedicanda International School becomes a beacon of excellence, contributing meaningfully to society while maintaining the highest moral and ethical standards.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop young men and women with active and creative minds, a sense of compassion for others, and the courage to act on their beliefs and dreams. We are committed to providing a world-class education that integrates academic rigour with Christian values, equipping every learner with the knowledge, skills, and character needed to make a meaningful difference in society.

Through innovative teaching methods, comprehensive curricula, and a nurturing environment, we prepare our students not just for academic success, but for life success — instilling in them the values of integrity, excellence, and service to others.

Our Core Values

Everything we do at Virgo Praedicanda is shaped by our core values. These principles are woven into every lesson, every interaction, and every opportunity we provide — ensuring that our students grow not just in knowledge, but in wisdom, integrity, and purpose.

Academic Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in teaching and learning, encouraging every student to reach their full potential.

Christian Character

We integrate Christian values into all aspects of education, developing students with strong moral foundations.

Faith in God

We nurture spiritual growth and encourage students to develop a personal relationship with God.

Love & Compassion

We teach empathy, kindness, and care for others, fostering a spirit of service and community.

Discipline

We instill self-control, responsibility, and the importance of making good choices.

Independence

We encourage critical thinking, creativity, and the confidence to make informed decisions.
