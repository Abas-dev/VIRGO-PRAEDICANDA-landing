About the School

Established in 2018, Virgo Praedicanda International School (VPIS) is an independent, Christian, co-educational day school committed to providing outstanding education for children from six weeks of age through the secondary school level.

Since its inception, the school has remained dedicated to nurturing academic excellence while developing sound moral values, leadership qualities, creativity, and lifelong learning skills that prepare learners for success in an ever-changing global society.

From the very beginning, VPIS has been structured to:

  • Operate as a co-educational institution offering quality education from Early Years through Nursery, Primary, and Secondary School.
  • Provide a standard of education comparable to leading schools in the United Kingdom, the United States of America, and Singapore.
  • Integrate the English National Curriculum with relevant aspects of the Nigerian Curriculum, ensuring a broad, balanced, and internationally-minded educational experience.
  • Foster a culture of academic excellence, innovation, discipline, leadership, and integrity in a safe, inclusive, and nurturing environment.

Founded on Christian principles and values, Virgo Praedicanda International School is dedicated to developing the whole child — academically, morally, socially, emotionally, physically, and spiritually. We believe that every child possesses unique talents and abilities, and our responsibility is to provide opportunities that enable each learner to discover, develop, and maximize their full potential.

Today, VPIS proudly serves families through its campuses located in Woji and Chinda, providing modern learning facilities, experienced educators, and a stimulating environment where learners are inspired to excel.

Our School Levels

Early Years & Nursery

Children are introduced to learning through engaging, play-based experiences that build confidence, curiosity, communication, and foundational literacy and numeracy skills.

Primary School

Pupils gain strong academic foundations while nurturing creativity, critical thinking, collaboration, and problem-solving. An enriched curriculum supported by technology, arts, sports, and co-curricular programmes.

Secondary School

Students are prepared for higher education and future careers through rigorous academics, leadership development, digital literacy, research, entrepreneurship, and character education.

Excellence in Every Aspect

At VPIS, excellence is reflected in every aspect of school life. Through highly qualified and dedicated teachers, innovative instructional practices, continuous assessment, and a learner-centred approach, we inspire every student to pursue excellence while embracing integrity, compassion, and service.

Beyond academics, we provide diverse opportunities for learners to discover and develop their talents through:

  • Sports, music, public speaking, and ballet
  • Coding, STEM education, and digital literacy
  • Leadership programmes, clubs, and competitions
  • Community service and enriching co-curricular activities

Our curriculum is intentionally designed to equip learners with the knowledge, skills, values, and global perspectives necessary to thrive in the twenty-first century. We strive to raise lifelong learners who are compassionate, confident, resilient, technologically proficient, and multi-skilled — critical thinkers who respect diversity, care for the environment, uphold strong ethical values, and make meaningful contributions to their communities and the world.
