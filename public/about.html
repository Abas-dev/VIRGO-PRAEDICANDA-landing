About us

The founding of Virgo Praedicanda International School was motivated by the desire to provide top-notch instruction. Our bond is our word, or what we pledge to our parents. The school is the pinnacle of achievement, teamwork, intelligence, discipline, and devotion.

Our vision is to raise children with strong, personable character equipped with knowledge that can compete globally. We inspire and nurture excellent children for global impact — developing confident, compassionate leaders who are rooted in faith and ready to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Who We Are

A School Built on Purpose

Established with a passion for excellence, Virgo Praedicanda International School (VPIS) is an independent, Christian, co-educational day school providing outstanding education from Early Years through Secondary School level.

Since its founding, VPIS has remained dedicated to nurturing academic excellence while developing sound moral values, leadership qualities, creativity, and lifelong learning skills that prepare every learner for success in an ever-changing global society.

  • Education from Early Years, Nursery, Primary through Secondary School.
  • Standards comparable to leading schools in the UK, USA, and Singapore.
  • English National Curriculum integrated with the Nigerian Curriculum.
  • A safe, inclusive, nurturing environment rooted in Christian values.
